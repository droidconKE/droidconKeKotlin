The official app for DroidconKe & FlutterconKe 2026, Africa's largest mobile developer conference, on 5 and 6 November in Nairobi.

Plan your conference
• See every session by day, with its time, room and speakers
• Filter by room, level and format, or show only the sessions you've starred
• Star sessions to build your agenda, and see straight away when two overlap
• Read the details of any session and share it

Never miss a session
• Get a reminder before each session you star, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes ahead
• Hear about room changes, delays and cancellations
• Get announcements from the organisers
• Choose what you're notified about

Your ticket, ready at the door
• Add your ticket with its number and the email you bought it with, or scan your printed ticket
• It works without a signal, and the screen brightens when you show it at the entrance

Meet the speakers
• Browse and search every speaker, and read their profiles
• See the sessions each speaker is giving

Stay in the loop
• Home counts down to the conference, then shows your agenda for the day
• Now and next shows the session that's on and the one coming up
• Updates lists changes to the sessions in your agenda
• Follow the conference feed
• Add the home screen widget to see your next session at a glance

Also
• Light and dark themes, and support for large text
• The schedule is saved on your phone, so it keeps working on a weak signal
• Send feedback to the organisers from the app

The app is built by the community and is open source: github.com/droidconKE/droidconKeKotlin
